Private operations access

Required for Question Bank, Blueprint, review, lookup, and export. Enter the already configured n8n shared secret only in your browser. It is never written into this file, displayed in a result, or sent to the Product Format activation route.

Load the live catalogue before choosing an upload or product lookup context.

New content upload

Question Bank ownership: select the active Course that owns the uploaded content. The server resolves the immutable Course identity before an upload log, validation, or content write.

Question Bank review

Review staged Question Bank rows before making eligible rows usable. Approval is explicit and preserves question text, answers, options, provenance, and taxonomy.

Blueprint approval desk

Set the Class and session once at the top, then list every Blueprint revision for that Class. Optionally narrow the view to one Product. A DRAFT can be approved by someone other than its recorded uploader, or opened as the basis for a new revision.

Product lookup and exports

Select an active Paper Product once, then look up its current Format, inspect Blueprint revisions, or export its latest approved Blueprint.

Final Product Format activation

Use only for a new, separately owner-approved replacement. The Exact-18 release receipt that activated import 11 is consumed. Do not re-upload the activated workbook or reuse its receipt.

Current V6 route: https://n8n.mytst.cloud/webhook/mytst-product-format-activate-nonprod
This route never creates Courses or Products, imports Blueprints, changes Question Bank content, or permits CBSE-12-IP.

The V6 contract performs definitive validation before any activation write.
